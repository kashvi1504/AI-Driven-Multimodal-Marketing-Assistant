FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data private?

Yes. Sentiment Studio runs on your own computer, and your ad copy and images are analyzed locally. The only things that leave your machine are the pages you ask the Website Audit to fetch, and the page URL sent to Google PageSpeed Insights for speed scores.

What does "heuristic" mean next to a score?

It means the score comes from a transparent set of rules (like "has a call to action" or "image is in focus") rather than a model trained on campaign results. We label it so you always know how much weight to give it, and every heuristic score lists the checks behind it.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization: how ready your page is to be found, understood and cited by AI assistants and AI search. The audit checks things like structured data, clear answers, and whether AI crawlers are allowed.

Do I need a powerful computer or a GPU?

No. Copy, image and website analysis run comfortably on a normal laptop. Generative Editing works on a CPU too, but a GPU makes it much faster.

Can it use my own trained models?

Yes. If you fine-tune the sentiment model or train the image engagement model on your own data, the app picks them up automatically and shows which one is in use.

Does it cost anything?

No subscription and no credits. It runs on your own machine, so the only cost is your computer's time.
